Vector databases store high-dimensional embeddings for similarity search.
Cosine similarity ranks vectors by the angle between them.
HNSW is a graph index that makes nearest-neighbor search fast.
Keyword search matches exact words while semantic search matches meaning.
Embeddings turn text into numbers a model can compare.
Pinecone, Weaviate, and Qdrant are popular vector databases.
Top K controls how many nearest matches a query returns.
Chunking splits long documents into pieces before embedding them.
RAG retrieves relevant context and feeds it to an LLM.
Recall measures how many of the relevant items a search actually finds.
